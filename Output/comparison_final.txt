
========================================
FINAL OPTIMIZED RESULTS (DistilBERT only)
========================================
Method 1 (TF-IDF + Deep MLP)               : 88.40%
Method 2 (Word2Vec + BiGRU)                : 90.85%
Method 3 (Frozen DistilBERT + LogReg)      : 84.28%
Method 4 (Few-shot, 20-shot, 10 trials)    : 66.57% (±4.10%)

Observations:
- TF-IDF + deep MLP achieves strong performance (~88.4%) with low computational cost.
- Word2Vec + BiGRU is slightly lower but captures word order.
- Frozen DistilBERT with strong regularization (weight decay=0.1) gives ~84.3%, avoiding overfitting.
- Few-shot provides reasonable accuracy without training, but varies with support set.
